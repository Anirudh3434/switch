Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

The short version

Switch reads the login state of websites you explicitly grant, encrypts it on your device, and never sends it anywhere in a form we can read. We don't sell data, we don't run analytics on your browsing, and we can't recover your sessions if you lose your password.

What Switch accesses

  • Cookies — for sites you grant, read when you click Save this session or Restore. Cookies are how a site knows you're logged in.
  • localStorage and session tokens — for the same sites, at the same moments. Many modern apps store their auth token here instead of a cookie.

Access is requested per site, through your browser's own permission prompt. Switch does not request access to all websites when installed, and does not read anything in the background.

What Switch never does

  • Never reads the passwords you type.
  • Never collects data from sites you haven't granted.
  • Never runs in the background watching your browsing.
  • Never sends your sessions, keys, or password to our servers.
  • Never sells or shares data with third parties.

How your data is protected

Sessions are encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a key derived from your password (PBKDF2-SHA256, 310,000 iterations). The key is derived separately for authentication and encryption, so a leaked login hash can't be used to decrypt your sessions. The server stores only encrypted blobs — it never holds your password or your encryption key. This is zero-knowledge: we cannot read your sessions even if compelled to.

Where data is stored

  • On your device — in the extension's local storage, encrypted. This is the default, and works without an account.
  • On our server — only if you sign in to sync across your own machines. Encrypted blobs only. You can delete them at any time.

Sharing with other people

If you choose to hand a session to a teammate, it's re-encrypted to their public key before upload. Only they can open it. We can't.

Your control

You can withdraw consent and delete all saved sessions at any time from the extension's settings. Withdrawing consent deletes every saved session, revokes site access, and cannot be undone.

What Switch can't do

Some sites (banks, Google, streaming services) bind sessions to a device or IP and will reject a session moved from another browser. Passkeys cannot be exported by design. We tell you when a restore is rejected rather than retrying silently.
