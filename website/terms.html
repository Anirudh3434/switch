2. What Switch is

Switch captures the login state (cookies and web storage) of websites you explicitly grant, encrypts it on your device, and lets you move or share that encrypted state. Sync across devices is optional.

Switch is pre-release software, supplied for evaluation and personal use. Features may change or be withdrawn.

Supported browsers

Switch currently targets Chromium-based browsers (Chrome, Edge, Brave and similar). Firefox is not supported: there is no Gecko build and no addons.mozilla.org listing. There is currently no published store listing for any browser; installation is from an unpacked developer build unless the operator states otherwise.
