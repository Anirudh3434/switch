Zero-Knowledge Architecture

How Switch Actually Works

Password managers stop at the login form. Switch operates at the authenticated session layer, moving live web logins between browsers without sending plain-text credentials across the wire.

The Cryptographic Transfer Pipeline

A rigorous 4-phase sequence engineered directly within the WebCrypto browser sandbox.

PHASE 01

Envelope Isolation

When authenticated on a service (Okta, GitHub, ChatGPT, AWS), the extension captures your tab's session state via standard chrome.cookies APIs, filtering domain cookies, httpOnly, and Secure flags into an isolated memory payload.

PHASE 02

Client-Side Sealing

Before touching any network pipe, WebCrypto executes PBKDF2 (100,000 iterations + SHA-256) over your Master Password to derive a symmetric AES-256-GCM key. The cookie payload is encrypted into opaque ciphertext. Our sync servers never see plaintext.

PHASE 03

Version Protection

Each synced envelope carries a monotonic baseVersion header. If a laptop that was asleep for three days tries to push an expired session, Switch detects the version divergence and protects your freshest credentials from collision.

PHASE 04

Kinetic Ingestion & Rollback

On the target browser, Switch decrypts the blob locally and snapshots existing cookies into an emergency rollback vault before injecting the fresh session. If you change your mind, hit "Restore" to revert immediately.

Why Cookie Envelopes Beat Password Fillers

Password managers stop at the login form. Switch operates at the session layer, eliminating the friction of secondary auth hurdles.

01

Bypass Multi-Factor Authentication Loops

When you authenticate with Okta, Google Workspace, GitHub, or AWS on your primary workstation, your browser holds the authenticated session cookie. Switch syncs that encrypted state so you never have to reach for a phone or hardware security key when switching browsers.

02

Instant 1-Click Rollback

Before Switch applies any remote session, it snapshots your current tab's cookies into a local backup record. Changed your mind or need your previous persona back? Hit "Restore Original Cookies" and the previous state is immediately reapplied.

03

Strict Version Conflict Resolution

Avoid session clobbering. Each website envelope carries a monotonic `baseVersion` header. If a laptop that was asleep for three days tries to push an expired session, Switch detects the version divergence and protects your freshest login.

04

Zero-Knowledge Cryptographic Boundary

Your master password never touches the network in plaintext. WebCrypto derives your authHash and AES-256 encryption keys via PBKDF2 (100,000 iterations + SHA-256). The sync backend only ever stores opaque ciphertext blobs. Even if our cloud database were inspected, your session cookies cannot be read.
