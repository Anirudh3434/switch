Engineered at the Session Layer

Password managers stop at the login form. Switch moves active authenticated states directly between browsers.

01 / SESSION CONTINUITY

Bypass Repetitive 2FA Challenges

Authenticate once on your primary workstation. Switch securely mirrors live sessions so you never reach for security keys or phones when changing browsers.

02 / ZERO KNOWLEDGE

Client-Sealed AES-256 Vault

Keys are derived via PBKDF2 (100,000 iterations) directly in your browser. Encrypted ciphertexts mean sync servers can never inspect your cookies.

03 / INSTANT ROLLBACK

Automatic Snapshot Backups

Switch automatically snapshots your current tab's cookies before any incoming injection, letting you revert to previous sessions with 1 click.
